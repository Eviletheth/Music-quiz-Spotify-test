Rules

 10 seconds of playback for 1- and 2-pointers · 20 seconds for 3 and up · Ask the host to sing a line: ±1 point (use the −1 / +1 buttons on the team cards) · Bonus after the category answer: +1 for the artist (+A) and +1 for the song title (+S) · Once every team has missed a tile it turns green and is open to the whole room · When the board is empty the bonus round starts: one standalone song per category, played in full until the host pauses it, worth whatever the host sets. · "Example round" up at the top is a throwaway tile on a pretend scoreboard - play it however you like, it changes nothing.
